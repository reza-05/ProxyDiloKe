# PROXY DILO KE? — The IUT Attendance Mystery
### CSE 4302: Object Oriented Programming Lab Project

---

## 📌 About The Game

**"Proxy Dilo Ke? The IUT Attendance Mystery"** is a terminal-based social deduction and forensic investigation game inspired by classic deduction games like Mafia and Among Us, customized with authentic university campus culture.

### 🎭 The Premise (Room 302 Mystery)
During an 8:00 AM class in Room 302, the teacher conducts roll call while looking down at the attendance register. He physically counts **40 students** sitting in the room, but records **45 verbal responses** shouting *"Present sir!"*. 

A secret **Proxy Syndicate** operated by an elusive student mastermind—the **Proxy King**—has imitated voices and called fake proxies for 5 absent friends from the back rows!

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

A squad of **1 to 4 student detectives** must interrogate classroom suspects, track statements across rounds, catch verbal contradictions, and cast an official expulsion vote to unmask the **Proxy King** before Round 4 ends.

### Key Game Features:
1. **Interactive Interrogation:** Question suspects with authentic student archetypes:
   - **The CR (Class Representative):** Managing attendance and standing near the podium.
   - **The Backbencher:** Chilling at the last bench, claiming to mind their own business.
   - **The Topper:** First row student focused on code and notes.
   - **The Sleeper:** Half-asleep on the desk under the AC.
   - **The Latecomer:** Bursting into class late with highway traffic jam excuses.
   - **The Gossip Master:** Observant student who tracks everyone entering and leaving.
2. **Contradiction Detection:** Honest students give consistent alibis across rounds. Members of the proxy syndicate fabricate stories and slip up between their true and fake whereabouts. Detectives can challenge these contradictions to earn investigation points.
3. **Classroom Trial & Vote:** At the end of the round, detectives vote to accuse a suspect. Exposing the Proxy King seals victory, while falsely accusing an innocent student incurs disciplinary point penalties.
